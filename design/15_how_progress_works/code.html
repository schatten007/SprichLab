Why Typed Notes Are NOT Speaking Evidence

When you type a German sentence, your fingers navigate mechanical keys while your visual cortex corrects misspellings on screen. You have ample time to ponder grammar rules in your working memory.

Spoken fluency is an entirely distinct physical event. It demands instantaneous breath control, rapid tongue placement against the palate for the German ‘ch’ and ‘r’, active vowel duration adjustments, and latency tolerance under social stress.

info

Typing is exceptional for grammar drills, orthography, and vocab indexing. But inside Sprich, only actual phonation through the vocal tract unlocks active speaking milestones.
