# Start speaking — a short guide

> Want the smallest possible routine, or have ADHD? Start with [Start small](START_SMALL.md). This page is the fuller walkthrough.

## Find your next lesson

Open **Lessons**. **My path** shows your current stage, one recommended lesson with a short reason, and progress for each of the five stages:

**Start speaking → Build sentences → Handle everyday situations → Keep conversations going → University and work**

Every lesson stays open. **All lessons** filters all 48 by level, topic, speaking skill, status, and search.

Levels describe the task, not your ability:

| Level | What the lesson asks of you |
| --- | --- |
| Starter | Say a short model phrase and change one detail |
| A1 | Answer familiar questions and manage a basic transaction |
| A2 | Add detail, give a simple reason, clarify, handle follow-ups |
| B1 | Explain, compare, and tell a short story |

Some lessons show **Useful first:** with links. That is advice, not a lock — you can always start anyway.

## Start with one small lesson

1. Open **http://localhost:8080/**. If the app is not already running, start it with `npm run dev` from the project folder.
2. Keep **Learning path → Start speaking · near-beginner** selected.
3. Start the recommended lesson, or choose a situation from **Lessons**.
4. Choose **Start guided** or **Try with less help**. Guided keeps the English meaning and a model answer one tap away; with less help both stay hidden until you ask. Both record support the same way.
5. Say the first model aloud. Then work through the words, the language focus, the short conversation, and your own replies.
6. Use the model answer whenever you need it, then choose **Needed help**. **Said it on my own** is for an aloud attempt without that answer hint.

Recording is optional. An installed German device voice provides free read-aloud; see [voice setup](FREE_SETUP.md#1-free-german-read-aloud--no-account-or-api-key).

Practising a lesson again uses its **other** model conversation and variation where one exists.

## Take the lesson to Gemini

Open **Export for Gemini** on the lesson overview, inside the lesson, or from its library card. You can export before finishing.

- **Copy practice pack:** paste the text into Gemini.
- **Download .txt:** keep or move a readable UTF-8 file to your phone.
- **Share:** use the native share menu on supported devices. If sharing is unavailable, copy or download still works.

The pack includes this lesson’s words, meanings, sentence patterns, dialogue, and variations, plus its **level target** and your chosen **help setting**. It asks Gemini to start with a short warm-up, then have a natural conversation with one short question at a time. Personal profile fields are included only when you check them.

**Computer → phone:** paste the pack into a normal Gemini text chat on your computer. On the same Google account, open that chat in the Gemini phone app and tap **Live**.

**Phone:** paste the pack into a normal Gemini chat, then tap **Live**. Say “Please start the warm-up from this lesson.” If you get stuck, try **Hilfe**, **langsamer**, or **noch einmal**.

Google documents [switching between text and Live in the same conversation](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15274899?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&hl=en). Features and limits depend on your Gemini app/account. Sprich’s export is local and needs no Gemini API key.

Active lesson recording is saved and stopped before export. Copying, downloading, or sharing does not grade an answer or finish a lesson.

## Learn useful words

Open **Words**. Search in German or English, choose a topic or word type, and save words you want to revisit.

Choose a practice mode:

1. **Recognise the meaning:** see German and recall the English meaning.
2. **Say the German word:** see the meaning and try the German aloud, including a noun’s article.
3. **Use it in a sentence:** make a short sentence of your own.

Choose **Learn five words** or **Review due words**. Try before revealing, then compare honestly. Revealing first keeps the attempt assisted. Recognition and spoken recall have separate review schedules.

The **Useful phrases & your own collection** link lets you keep additional expressions with their English meaning and your own example.

## Keep a takeaway

After Gemini, use **Save Gemini takeaway** on the lesson or export page. Keep a sentence you managed, something to practise, or a useful new word. Find it later in **Progress**. Gemini minutes are self-reported and kept separate from measured in-app time.

For offline use, use the production/hosted app and open the beginner course once while online. The course is cached on the device; saved sessions also carry their own lesson content. Use **Settings → Export progress** to move learner progress between devices. Recordings and the downloaded course cache are device-local.
